# GitReel — Hype Visual Elements

TikTok-style energy ideas beyond standard data visualization. The goal: dopamine delivery, not infographics.

---

## Core TikTok Principles

1. **Beat sync** -- every visual punch lands on a beat drop
2. **Speed ramping** -- slow-mo on flex moments, hyperspeed through the grind
3. **Big numbers going brrr** -- fast-counting odometers are inherently satisfying
4. **"Wait for it" tension** -- buildup before a reveal
5. **Screen shake / camera punch** -- physical impact on big moments
6. **Achievement unlock / gaming UI** -- devs are gamers, this language works

---

## Scene Ideas

### "Numbers Go Crazy" Counter Wall

Full-screen rapid-fire stat counters that slam in one after another with screen shake:
- `127,493` LINES OF CODE (counts up from 0 at insane speed)
- `2,847` COMMITS (slam)
- `189` CONTRIBUTORS (slam)
- `14.2K` STARS (slam with gold particle explosion)

Each number fills the entire screen, bold af, with a chromatic aberration flash on impact. Think sports broadcast stat graphics meets Mr. Beast thumbnails.

### "The Grind" Commit Speedrun

Hyperspeed montage of commit messages scrolling like end credits at 100x speed, with occasional slow-mo "pause" on funny/significant commits. Commit counter in corner going absolutely wild. Date flying forward. The feeling of "look how much work went into this."

### Achievement Unlock Pop-ups

Gaming-style toast notifications that slam in during the timelapse:
- "FIRST COMMIT" (bronze badge)
- "100 COMMITS" (silver, with particle burst)
- "1000 COMMITS" (gold, screen shake)
- "100 STARS" (star explosion)
- "FIRST EXTERNAL CONTRIBUTOR" (special effect)
- "10K LINES" / "100K LINES"
- "1 YEAR OLD" (birthday cake icon)
- "STREAK: 30 DAYS" (fire icon)

Turns the repo history into a **speedrun** with milestone callouts.

### "Hot Files" Flame Effect

During treemap timelapse, files getting changed a lot literally glow/pulse with a fire/heat effect. The more commits touch a file, the hotter it burns. Visual story: "this is where the real work happened."

### Before/After Dramatic Reveal

Split screen or swipe-wipe:
- LEFT: Day 1 -- sad little `README.md` and `index.js`, two lonely rectangles
- RIGHT: Today -- massive treemap, hundreds of files, full ecosystem

The wipe happens on a beat drop. Maximum flex energy.

### Contributor "Boss Entry"

Top contributor's avatar zooms in from nothing with a shockwave ring effect (like anime power-up). Their stats appear: "452 commits, 89,000 lines". Then second contributor enters slightly smaller. Third even smaller. Like a fighting game character select screen but with commit stats.

### "Typing" Code Reveal

Actual code snippets from the repo (first lines of the main entry file, or the most-starred file) typing out character by character with a terminal cursor, monospace font, syntax highlighting. Purely vibes -- makes it feel like a real project, not just charts.

### Star Explosion Counter

Stars literally rain down from the top of the screen like confetti. The number in the center counts up. At milestones (100, 1K, 10K) there's a massive burst. Think: mobile game reward screen.

### Language War / Race Bar

Animated bar chart race (like those GDP-by-country videos) but for languages over time. TypeScript overtakes JavaScript. Rust creeps up. Python dominates. People LOVE bar chart races -- they're inherently competitive and watchable.

### "Wrapped" Spotify-Style Stats Card

End card that looks like Spotify Wrapped:
- "Your repo had more commits than 94% of open source projects"
- "Busiest day: 47 commits on March 15"
- "Most dedicated: @john with a 45-day streak"
- "Languages spoken: 7"

This is the **screenshot moment** -- the thing people actually share.

## Effect Layers (Apply Across All Scenes)

### Glitch Transitions

Between every scene: RGB split, VHS tracking lines, digital noise burst lasting 3-5 frames. Gives the video that edited/produced feel rather than slideshow energy.

### Screen Shake / Camera Punch

On every "impact" moment (number slam, achievement unlock, big commit, beat drop), the entire frame shifts 5-10px in a random direction and springs back. Separates "professional motion graphics" from "animated PowerPoint."

### Particle System Layer

Ambient floating particles (small dots, code characters, or mini stars) that drift across the screen throughout the video. Reacts to "impact" moments by scattering outward. Adds depth and life to every scene.

### Speed Lines / Radial Blur

During fast-forward sections of the timelapse, manga-style speed lines radiating from center. Communicates velocity and urgency.

## Suggested Hype Sequence (30-45s)

```
[0-2s]    Black -> repo name SLAMS in with glitch + bass hit
[2-4s]    Numbers Go Crazy wall (stars, commits, contributors rapid fire)
[4-8s]    Before/After split reveal on beat drop
[8-30s]   Treemap timelapse with:
            - Achievement unlocks popping in
            - Hot file glow effects
            - Speed ramping (slow on big moments)
            - Screen shake on big commits
            - Contributor avatars flashing on commits
            - Ambient particles
[30-34s]  Language War bar chart race (compressed, fast)
[34-37s]  Contributor Boss Entry (top 3)
[37-42s]  Spotify Wrapped stats card
[42-45s]  Outro: "Generate yours -> gitreel.dev"
```
